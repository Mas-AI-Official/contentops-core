# Research rules

Lead fields: business/location/category, canonical website, discovery source, observations with URLs/dates, hypothesis, proposed intervention, evidence/fit score, public contact/source, consent status/basis/evidence, suppression flag, stage, next question and owner.

Use null for unknowns. Keep Maps-derived content separate from independently obtained first-party facts. Places IDs have a documented retention exception; check current terms before storing other Maps content. Request only required fields and cap Places spending.

For Canadian outreach verify current CASL rules for the actual message/channel. Conspicuous publication depends on required publication conditions and role relevance; an unsubscribe link does not cure missing consent. Store evidence/expiry. Include applicable sender identification/contact and unsubscribe handling. Do not publish the owner's private home address without specific authority.

Do not certify legal compliance from an agent-supplied boolean. Review actual evidence. Unknown basis stays ready-to-review.

Example: a business lists two different contact domains (observed); customers may be uncertain (hypothesis); ask which inbox receives enquiries (validation); offer corrected routing/intake (intervention). Do not invent a dollar loss.

Reject sites explicitly advertised for rent as unverified operating prospects. Do not call an existing booking integration missing merely because a clinic also lists a phone.
