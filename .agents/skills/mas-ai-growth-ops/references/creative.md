# Offers and creative

Apply Hormozi's value framework qualitatively: clarify the desired customer result, make delivery evidence credible, shorten time to useful progress, and reduce adoption effort. It is not a sales prediction formula.

Connect a real bottleneck to a concrete intervention and working demonstration. Record audience, observed problem, desired result, mechanism, deliverables, responsibilities, assumptions, proof and one CTA. Prices, urgency and guarantees require approved records. Never fabricate scarcity or outcomes.

Use AIDA/problem-solution or objection-handling structures when useful; do not mechanically repeat one script. A good MAS-AI example shows intake-to-CRM work and invites a bottleneck discussion. A bad example pastes an unrelated news headline onto an agent script and claims fivefold speed without evidence.

Study creator examples with Observed / Creator-stated / Inferred / Unknown labels. Separate organic, sponsored and paid content. Compare similar topic/age/duration cohorts. Pinned cumulative views cannot establish a winning format or posting hour. Annotate actual footage for motion conclusions.

A cover should show the subject and benefit: clear product/logo, approved portrait or product visual, short headline and one emphasis colour. Use original MAS-AI branding and composition.
